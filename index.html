Resume Enhancing & Job Searching AI Tool

Description

Resumes get left-swiped in a fraction of a second. Companies embed ATS tools into their hiring pipelines to filter candidates before a human ever sees the CV. Curious about how these work under the hood, I built and ran the whole thing myself. The result is a tool that reviews your CV like a real ATS would, giving you a match score, keyword gaps, tailored suggestions, auto-generated cover letters, LinkedIn outreach messages, and job search results matched to your profile. Everything runs locally on Ollama (7B/9B/14B models) on a consumer GPU with no data ever leaving the machine.

Feature Breakdown

  • CV + JD Upload: Upload your CV (PDF) and any job description; the tool extracts and structures your resume data automatically.
  • Keyword Matching: Shows which JD keywords are present in your CV, including synonym matches.
  • Gap & Partial Match Analysis: Displays missing skills, overall match %, and flags JD requirements that only partially match.
  • Optimization Suggestions: Actionable resume edits generated specifically against the target JD.
  • Cover Letter Generation: Auto-generates a tailored cover letter using your matching skills.
  • LinkedIn Message Generator: Auto-generates outreach/connection messages, with tone and content customization.
  • AI-Suggested Priority Actions: LLM ranks which actions matter most to improve your JD match.
  • Smart Job Search: Uses your CV + extracted keywords to search and filter live LinkedIn jobs matching your profile.
  • Direct Apply from UI: All job links verified and valid, apply without leaving the tool.

Technical Architecture

  • Local LLM Inference: Self-hosted via Ollama, handles keyword extraction, suggestions, cover letters & LinkedIn messages offline.
  • Semantic Matching: Sentence-transformer embeddings compute cosine similarity between JD and CV content.
  • Bidirectional Verification: Every matched keyword is cross-checked against both the CV and JD before being counted.
  • Hybrid Scoring Engine: ATS score computed via a weighted formula (exact + semantic matches - missing requirements).
  • Job Discovery Pipeline: Extracted skills feed a live job-scraping and filtering layer, ranking listings by CV relevance.

Technologies: Ollama • LLM (7B/9B/14B) • Semantic Embeddings • Python • ATS Scoring • Resume Parsing
